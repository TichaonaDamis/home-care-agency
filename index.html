♥CareHours

Care management for Adult Family Homes & home care agencies

Scheduling and timekeeping, daily care documentation, medications, health monitoring, incidents, hospital & hospice transitions, payroll, billing and monthly reports, with role-based access for administrators, nurses and caregivers.

Try it now

The live demo runs the complete application in your browser with fictional sample data. Nothing is sent to a server, so do not enter real client information. Sign in as admin, sample.nurse or caregiver maria.sample with password Demo-Pass-2026.

For real use, CareHours v2 is a secure server application with a database, so it cannot run on GitHub Pages, which only serves static files. Deploy it to a host with a persistent disk and HTTPS. The README has step-by-step instructions (Render blueprint and Dockerfile included).

For administrators & managers

  • Client & caregiver registration
  • Assignments, calendar & coverage gaps
  • Timesheet approval & payroll estimates
  • Client invoices, payments & expenses
  • Monthly health, activity, financial & operations reports
  • Users, permissions & audit log

For nurses & clinical staff

  • Versioned care plans
  • Medication orders & change history
  • Vital-sign trends with client-specific thresholds
  • Progress indicators with supporting evidence
  • Alerts, incidents & care transitions

For caregivers

  • Today's and upcoming shifts
  • Clock in / out
  • Care plan & acknowledgements
  • ADLs, meals, activities, observations, medications
  • Incident reporting & own timesheet
